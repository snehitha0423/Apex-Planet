### Hypothesis Testing

The statistical analysis focused on determining whether there was a statistically significant difference between the average sales of male and female customers.

### Statistical Result

The independent two-sample t-test produced a p-value of approximately **0.4950**.

The significance level used was **0.05**.

Since:

**p-value = 0.4950 > 0.05**

the null hypothesis was not rejected.

Therefore, the difference between male and female average sales was **not statistically significant**.

### Business Interpretation

Gender alone should not be considered a strong standalone factor for sales targeting because the observed difference in average sales was not statistically significant.

Business decisions should instead consider multiple customer and business attributes such as:

- Customer behaviour
- Product preferences
- Category performance
- City
- Customer segment
- Revenue contribution

# Key Business Insights

The analysis of the sales dataset revealed several important findings:

- Electronics generated the highest category revenue.
- Laptop was a leading product by revenue.
- Patna recorded the highest city-wise sales.
- Electronics had the highest number of orders.
- Monthly revenue showed fluctuations across the year.
- Male and female customers had similar average sales.
- Customer segmentation revealed differences in revenue contribution across customer groups.
- Statistical testing showed that the difference between male and female average sales was not statistically significant.
- Business decisions should therefore consider multiple customer and business attributes rather than relying on gender alone.

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following business recommendations were identified:

### 1. Strengthen High-Performing Categories

Electronics generated the highest revenue and order volume.

The business should continue supporting strong-performing categories through:

- Inventory planning
- Promotional campaigns
- Product availability
- Targeted marketing

### 2. Prioritize High-Performing Products

Leading products such as laptops contributed significantly to revenue.

The business can focus on:

- Maintaining adequate inventory
- Product-specific promotions
- Cross-selling opportunities
- Customer offers

### 3. Focus on High-Performing Cities

Patna recorded the highest city-wise sales.

Successful strategies used in high-performing cities can be studied and potentially replicated in other regions.

### 4. Improve Lower-Performing Categories

Lower-performing categories can be improved through:

- Targeted promotions
- Customer offers
- Product bundling
- Better product positioning
- Demand analysis

### 5. Use Multi-Attribute Customer Segmentation

The statistical analysis showed that gender alone was not a statistically significant factor in average sales.

Therefore, customer segmentation should consider multiple attributes such as:

- Customer segment
- Product preference
- Category preference
- City
- Purchase behaviour
- Revenue contribution
